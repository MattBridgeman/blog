Earlier this year I redesigned my site. It was the first time since university that I had undertaken a full redesign of the site and I started from the ground up. Everything from server infrastructure to front-end code was completely overhauled. Overall I'm happy with the new look and feel, however I still felt the blog content was lacking and so as of today I am starting afresh.

Overall I felt the previous content of the blog lacked an overarching theme. The blog spanned about 4 years of my life from starting university to beginning life as a professional web developer. Everything from individual university assignments to my 'top 5 games of all time' were themes for blog posts. The goal of my site redesign was to focus on me as a web developer and I want the blog to reflect that too.

So the theme of my blog moving forwards will be to reflect my thoughts, experiments and experiences in web. In years to come hopefully I'll be able to reflect on the content and see a transformation of the technologies, workflows and opinions I have towards web development.
